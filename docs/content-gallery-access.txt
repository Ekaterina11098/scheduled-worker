This operation performs a fresh read-only China catalog query, selects isolated product photographs, retains original foreground RGB, caches alpha cutouts by source and implementation SHA-256, and assembles a deterministic 1086x1448 gallery. It does not reconstruct hidden product details or call a generative model.

Layout: neutral #f4f4f5; Russian title; three columns; up to fourteen items per page; fourteen means four complete rows and a centered pair. A following six uses the first two rows of the same grid and body scale. Only the corresponding China nmID appears below each product. Other family sizes use their actual fresh card count, never invented variants.
